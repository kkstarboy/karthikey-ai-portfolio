Personas — Evidence Insufficiency Report

Decision

Credible personas cannot be produced from the supplied evidence. The available BRD names three consumer groups and assigns needs to them, but supplies no traceable direct research, reliable behavioral data, participant evidence, analytics, support corpus, or customer-feedback source. Creating names, ages, biographies, photographs, quotations, behaviors, motivations, frustrations, or demographic detail would invent user evidence.

Confidence vocabulary: HIGH, MEDIUM, LOW, CONTRADICTORY. Candidate groups are LOW-confidence business context, not personas.

Research required before persona synthesis

  1. Confirm which product/design decisions personas must inform; do not create personas as decorative profiles.
  2. Recruit across candidate contexts and across literacy, language, accessibility, device/connectivity, account type, shared-finance, and financial-vulnerability contexts without stereotype-based quotas.
  3. Conduct consented recent-event interviews and contextual inquiry focused on current behavior, workarounds, triggers, trust, errors, privacy, and outcomes.
  4. Triangulate with governed analytics, category-correction behavior, support themes, and customer feedback using stable evidence IDs.
  5. Test whether candidate distinctions change decisions and whether groups should merge, split, or be replaced.
  6. Preserve negative cases and contradictions; report prevalence only when sampling and denominators justify it.

Review guidance
