Accessibility Review — Diagnostic, Partial, and Not a Conformance Claim

Evidence boundary and severity method

This review evaluates the existing two-screen HTML sample and Phase 09 specifications by source inspection, visual inspection of two captures, and contrast calculation. It does not establish accessibility or WCAG conformance.

  • Required standard, level, jurisdiction, and platform: Not supplied.
  • Diagnostic reference: W3C WCAG 2.2, including SC 1.4.3, accessed 2026-07-24: https://www.w3.org/TR/WCAG22/.
  • Target devices, browsers, assistive technologies, operating systems, accessibility settings, and disabled-participant research: Not supplied/not conducted.
  • Rendered/automated/manual/AT testing in Phase 09: Not conducted. Source and static captures only.
  • Severity labels: Critical = blocks or risks sensitive/core access; High = likely material barrier or misleading financial state; Medium = partial access/recovery burden; Low = limited localized effect. Severity is a design-risk assessment, not a participant finding.
  • Evidence boundary: HIGH confidence describes inspected code/asset facts or documented absence; it does not describe user impact prevalence or compliance.

2. Checklist and findings

A11Y-01: Primary button text contrast

  • Criterion/guideline: WCAG 2.2 SC 1.4.3 diagnostic reference.
  • Screen/component: Overview “Set a budget” and budget-sheet Save; .primary/.save-budget.
  • Issue: White normal-size text on #ff385c calculates to 3.52:1. If WCAG 2.2 Level AA normal-text contrast is adopted, this is below its 4.5:1 threshold.
  • Affected users: People with low vision or reduced contrast sensitivity.
  • Severity: High/blocking for handoff.
  • Evidence/check method: Direct CSS values plus relative-luminance calculation; HIGH confidence for values.
  • Recommended design behavior: Use a verified accessible text-bearing primary treatment. White on supplied darker #e00b41 calculates to 4.89:1; recheck all rendered default/hover/active/disabled/focus states before adoption.
  • Owner/status: Design-system and accessibility owners / Open.
  • Retest needed: Yes—rendered contrast and state regression.

A11Y-02: Budget choice state is not programmatically exposed

  • Criterion/guideline: Name, role, value/state; color/non-text cue independence.
  • Screen/component: Budget preset buttons.
  • Issue: Selection is represented by a .selected class and visual styling; source does not expose aria-pressed, radio semantics, or a checked state.
  • Affected users: Screen-reader, speech-input, and some cognitive-access users.
  • Severity: High.
  • Evidence/check method: Direct source inspection/HIGH.
  • Recommended design behavior: Use an approved radio/segmented-choice component with group label, one current value, keyboard behavior, and selected state exposed programmatically.
  • Owner/status: Design-system/accessibility/engineering / Open.
  • Retest needed: Yes—keyboard plus screen reader.

A11Y-03: Dialog focus and dismissal are incomplete

  • Criterion/guideline: Keyboard access, focus order/containment, accessible modal operation.
  • Screen/component: Budget modal/sheet.
  • Issue: Initial focus, Escape, and focus return exist, but focus is not trapped, background content is not made inert, and there is no explicit visible Close/Cancel control. Scrim click and Escape do not cover touch, voice, or switch needs.
  • Affected users: Keyboard, switch, screen-reader, voice-control, and motor-access users.
  • Severity: High.
  • Evidence/check method: Direct markup/script inspection/HIGH.
  • Recommended design behavior: Use a tested native/library dialog pattern with visible cancel/close, focus containment, inert background, predictable reading order, and return focus.
  • Owner/status: Design-system/accessibility/engineering / Open.
  • Retest needed: Yes—keyboard, switch, multiple screen readers, touch/voice where in scope.

A11Y-04: Focus destination differs from the interaction specification

  • Criterion/guideline: Focus order and understandable context after navigation.
  • Screen/component: Overview/detail screen switch.
  • Issue: showScreen() focuses the first button, while Phase 06 INT-03 calls for focus on the destination heading. The destination may be announced without its page context.
  • Affected users: Screen-reader, keyboard, switch, and magnification users.
  • Severity: Medium.
  • Evidence/check method: Source-to-specification comparison/HIGH.
  • Recommended design behavior: Choose and test a stable route-change pattern: focus a programmatically focusable page heading or announce the route with equivalent context, without creating an extra tab stop.
  • Owner/status: Accessibility/product engineering / Open.
  • Retest needed: Yes.

A11Y-05: Financial lists lack explicit list semantics

  • Criterion/guideline: Structure, relationships, and navigation.
  • Screen/component: Category rows and recent transactions.
  • Issue: Groups are generic div containers; relationship/order may be less efficient to navigate or announce than semantic lists. Only one category row is a button, so visual similarity masks differing interactivity.
  • Affected users: Screen-reader and cognitive-access users.
  • Severity: Medium.
  • Evidence/check method: Direct source/visual comparison/HIGH.
  • Recommended design behavior: Use list/list-item structure, make interaction availability explicit, and avoid visually identical interactive and static rows unless states are clear.
  • Owner/status: Product design/engineering/accessibility / Open.
  • Retest needed: Yes.

A11Y-06: Responsive zoom, reflow, and text scaling are unverified

  • Criterion/guideline: Resize text, reflow, orientation, readable content.
  • Screen/component: All sample screens.
  • Issue: The sample has a fixed staged 390px phone and one 760px breakpoint. No 200%/400% zoom, text-only scaling, short viewport, orientation, long-label, localized-script, or extreme-value evidence exists.
  • Affected users: Low-vision, magnification, language/localization, and constrained-device users.
  • Severity: High until tested.
  • Evidence/check method: CSS inspection and missing-test inventory/HIGH for absence.
  • Recommended design behavior: Approve platform/viewport/localization targets; test without clipped values/actions, two-dimensional scrolling for ordinary content, or lost focus/status.
  • Owner/status: Accessibility/product design/engineering/localization / Open.
  • Retest needed: Yes.

A11Y-07: Status feedback is transient and incomplete for financial actions

  • Criterion/guideline: Status messages, error identification, consistent recovery, sufficient time.
  • Screen/component: Toast and simulated budget save.
  • Issue: A polite live region exists, but messages disappear after 2.2 seconds and do not provide a durable authoritative state, history, retry, or error path. “Saved” is simulated without persistence.
  • Affected users: Screen-reader, cognitive/attention, low-vision, and users who need more time.
  • Severity: High for a real financial mutation; prototype limitation today.
  • Evidence/check method: Direct JS inspection plus Phase 07 coverage/HIGH.
  • Recommended design behavior: Keep transient announcement only as supplementary feedback; provide persistent result state with exact effect, retry/cancel/help, and authoritative status.
  • Owner/status: Product/data/engineering/accessibility / Open.
  • Retest needed: Yes across pending/success/failure/unknown/offline paths.

A11Y-08: Icon system and current navigation state need semantic review

  • Criterion/guideline: Non-text content, name/role/state, consistent identification.
  • Screen/component: Unicode glyph icons, tab navigation, status-bar symbols.
  • Issue: Interactive labels exist, but decorative glyphs within buttons are not consistently hidden, current-page state is visual only, and symbol rendering varies by font/platform.
  • Affected users: Screen-reader, speech-input, cognitive-access, and low-vision users.
  • Severity: Medium.
  • Evidence/check method: Direct source inspection/HIGH.
  • Recommended design behavior: Use approved icons, hide decorative glyphs, expose current state programmatically, and preserve explicit text labels.
  • Owner/status: Design-system/accessibility/engineering / Open.
  • Retest needed: Yes.

A11Y-09: Missing states prevent a complete accessibility evaluation

  • Criterion/guideline: Errors, instructions, status, authentication, consistent help, accessible authentication, and safe recovery as applicable.
  • Screen/component: WF-00–17 and WF-20–22.
  • Issue: Loading, empty, partial, stale, permission, validation, failure, conflict, offline, re-auth, export, recurring, and unusual-pattern flows have no high-fidelity implementation.
  • Affected users: All users; disproportionate impact is possible for disabled users.
  • Severity: Critical readiness blocker.
  • Evidence/check method: Phase 06–08 coverage comparison/HIGH for absence.
  • Recommended design behavior: Create and evaluate every required state after rules and risk controls are approved.
  • Owner/status: Product/design/engineering/accessibility/privacy/security / Blocked.
  • Retest needed: Yes.

Text review findings:

  • The sample uses readable, non-judgmental prose, but comprehension has not been tested.
  • Several labels use 11–13px text; text size alone is not declared nonconforming, but scaling, line height, fallback metrics, and localization require rendered review.

  • Do not use color, arrows, position, or animation alone for increase/decrease, selection, current navigation, or risk state.
