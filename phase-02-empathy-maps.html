Implications

  • Treat monthly summary, correction, recurring review, and nudges as testable concepts rather than proven solutions.
  • Research current behavior before asking for feature preferences.
  • Include privacy, accessibility, literacy, language, device/connectivity, and financial-vulnerability considerations.
  • Do not use these frames as evidence that a person thinks, feels, says, or does anything listed.
