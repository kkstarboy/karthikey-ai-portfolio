6. Heatmap limitations

  • No heatmap, session replay, click map, scroll map, attention map, page/state map, tool export, sample, capture period, or consent record was supplied.
  • Heatmaps may provide directional aggregate patterns only; they do not demonstrate user intent, attention, comprehension, satisfaction, causation, task success, or accessibility.
  • Session-level personal, account, financial, free-text, authentication, or other sensitive data must not be exposed. Masking, redaction, consent, access, retention, deletion, and vendor rules require human approval and testing.
  • Comparing maps across changed layouts, traffic sources, devices, consent populations, or instrumentation versions requires qualification.
  • Small or self-selected samples and aggregation can hide keyboard, screen-reader, zoom, reflow, localization, assistive-technology, and low-frequency high-severity barriers.
  • Until the above controls exist, heatmap capture and interpretation are blocked.
