5. Reduced-motion behavior

  1. Respect platform/user reduced-motion settings from first render; do not require a user to rediscover the preference inside the feature.
  2. Replace screen translate/scale, modal rise, chart growth, decorative pulse, and toast travel with immediate state changes or minimal non-spatial opacity only after review.
  3. Keep headings, focus, text, status roles, selected/current states, progress, and result content identical; reduced motion must not remove information.
  4. Do not auto-advance guided review steps, hide critical messages on animation completion, or delay interactive readiness for motion.
  5. Stop/reduce continuous decorative animation independently of task motion.
  6. Test reduced motion with keyboard, screen reader, zoom/reflow, slow device, and interrupted async states in supported platforms.

The sample CSS near-zero duration is directly observable, but compatibility and complete functional equivalence are untested.
