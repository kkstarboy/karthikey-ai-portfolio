1. Prioritization criteria

An evidence-constrained MoSCoW-style framework is used because RICE cannot be calculated without reach, impact evidence, confidence data, and effort estimates. Classifications are Proposed only:

Class Meaning in this artifact
Foundation before commitment Evidence, governance, safety, or feasibility work needed before a production priority
Candidate Must BRD capability central to the proposed value; still requires validation and owner approval
Candidate Should Supports value but sequencing depends on evidence, effort, risk, and dependencies
Candidate Could Plausible later option with unresolved need/scope
Decision needed Cannot be responsibly classified from current evidence

Criteria assessed separately: user outcome, business alignment, evidence confidence, financial/privacy/accessibility risk, dependency, effort knowledge, urgency, and validation need. No numeric score is used because false precision would imply unavailable data.

8. Validation plan

  1. Assign product, research, data, engineering, privacy/security/legal, accessibility, and content decision owners.
  2. Validate candidate problems and journeys with consented, inclusive direct research and governed behavioral/support evidence.
  3. Define the release horizon, capacity, scoring scales, effort ranges, risk thresholds, and mandatory work.
  4. Have owners independently rate user impact, business impact, reach, effort, risk, dependency, urgency, and confidence before facilitation.
  5. Reclassify the backlog through a recorded cross-functional session; preserve dissent and sensitivity ranges.
  6. Require human review for finance, privacy, accessibility, policy, high-risk, and production commitments.
