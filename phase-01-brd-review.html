4.2 Executive Summary

The BRD defines a coherent mobile expense-tracking concept with ten functional requirements, five risks, candidate metrics, and four scenarios. It does not establish the provenance of quoted user statements, validate the named target users, define data and integration rules, or provide measurable security, privacy, accessibility, and acceptance requirements.

Overall readiness: Needs major revisions. The concept is suitable for discovery, but not yet sufficiently testable or traceable for implementation approval.

6. Final Review Summary

  • Readiness: Needs major revisions because core data, rule, security, accessibility, integration, research-provenance, and acceptance details are missing.
  • Strongest areas: Clear product concept, supportive tone, representative scenarios, numbered functional requirements, candidate risks, and candidate success targets.
  • Critical blockers: No validated user-research basis; missing data/privacy/security/integration contracts; missing business rules; incomplete measurable NFRs and acceptance criteria.
  • Top five actions: validate user evidence; approve MVP scope; define data/privacy/security controls; specify categorization/budget/recurring rules; link requirements to acceptance criteria and metric definitions.
  • Open decisions: all questions in §14 plus owners, compliance basis, accessibility target, categorization accuracy definition, and error/recovery behavior.
  • Suggested sequence: product/research review → data/privacy/security review → product/engineering rules workshop → accessibility/content review → QA/analytics traceability review → stakeholder approval.
