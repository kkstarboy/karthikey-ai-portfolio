9. Validation plan

  1. Confirm parent app architecture, scope, global navigation, role/account model, and ownership.
  2. Resolve category, data, privacy/security, alert, export, and dynamic-state rules.
  3. Pilot the card set and conduct the authorized hybrid sort in the Phase 04 study plan.
  4. Revise labels/grouping with a traceable decision log; preserve context differences.
  5. Tree test realistic tasks T-01–T-06 with accessible tooling and defined success/directness metrics.
  6. Test contextual cross-links and recovery in task-flow usability work.
  7. Review with product, engineering, content, accessibility, privacy/security/legal, support, and data owners before production commitment.
