6. Localization and accessibility needs

  • Define locale-aware currency, date, number, category, merchant, percentage, and period formatting.
  • Do not concatenate dynamic financial sentences in ways that prevent grammar or screen-reader clarity.
  • Provide semantic labels, meaningful headings, text alternatives for charts, reading order, status announcements, and non-color cues.
  • Support text scaling/reflow, contrast, motor target size, keyboard/switch access where applicable, reduced motion, and plain-language explanations.
  • Research multilingual, literacy, device, connectivity, and assisted-service contexts before finalizing labels.
  • Treat privacy and error content as essential, not optional footer/help copy.

All items are LOW-confidence requirements candidates pending authorized standards and testing.
