Recommendations

  1. Define the product questions and decisions before requesting data.
  2. Supply event definitions, provenance, collection dates, denominators, cohort rules, consent/privacy basis, and data-quality checks.
  3. Supply deduplicated support and feedback sources with channel, date, product version, severity, and resolution metadata.
  4. Analyze contradictions across behavioral data, support evidence, direct research, and stakeholder claims; do not average them away.
  5. Establish guardrails for trust, correction burden, unwanted notifications, accessibility, and privacy—not engagement alone.
