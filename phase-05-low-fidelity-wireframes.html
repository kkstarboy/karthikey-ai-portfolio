Low-Fidelity Wireframes — Provisional Structural Exploration

Evidence basis and fidelity boundary

These text wireframes compare structural approaches before visual styling. They are not high-fidelity screens, measured layouts, interactive prototypes, user-tested designs, or implementation specifications.

  • Primary problem: Spending-period comprehension plus category recovery.
  • Concepts explored: C-01 Overview First and C-02 Guided Review; C-03 is represented in a narrow contextual correction variant.
  • Sources: Phase 05 concepts/review; Phase 04 content, sitemap, flows; Phase 03 needs/problems; BRD.
  • Confidence: All layouts/transitions are Inferred/LOW. BRD sample amounts are labeled content-format examples, not actual user data.
  • Constraints/gaps: Small-screen mobile intent; exact viewport, parent app shell, platform, components, business/data rules, permissions, accessibility target, localization, and feasibility are Unknown.

1. Concept goals

Goal ID Structural question Concepts Evidence Success to test
CG-01 Can a person understand period, total, categories, and data status? C-01/C-02 N-01, F-01 Accurate explanation without prompting
CG-02 Can a person find and recover from a wrong category? C-01/C-03 N-02, F-02 Locate, cancel/confirm, understand future effect
CG-03 Can guidance reduce complexity without removing control? C-02 N-04/N-06 Skip/back/full-view understood
CG-04 Are privacy, freshness, uncertainty, and errors visible? All N-05, alternate states Safe interpretation/recovery

└──────────────────────────────────────┘
  • Scenario/user goal: J-03/F-01, understand and freely choose a next step.
  • Problem addressed: N-01/N-04/N-06, PS-03.
  • Key idea: One question at a time with permanent skip/exit/full-view control.
  • Assumptions: System can safely choose sequence/relevance; progress need not use a fixed number; no action is a valid end.
  • States: No comparison history, no relevant change, partial data, stale insight, deep-link error, resumed session.
  • Questions: Does sequence clarify or patronize? Do users notice Full view/Exit? Does optional action feel neutral?
  • Links: C-02; F-01/F-06; BRD FR1/5–8.
