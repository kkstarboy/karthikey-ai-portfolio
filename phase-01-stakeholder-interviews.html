Core Interview Guide

Business Context

  • Which outcomes are mandatory for the first release, and who owns each decision?
  • What is explicitly out of scope, and what happens if the feature is not launched?

User Understanding

  • What direct research supports the three consumer groups, needs, and quotations in BRD §§2, 4, and 5?
  • Which groups, contexts, literacy/access needs, and edge cases have not been studied?

Current and Future State

  • How do users currently understand and correct transaction categories? Provide recent specific examples.
  • What should remain manual, become automated, or require confirmation?

Functional Rules and Failure Paths

  • How are merchants/categories identified, and what happens when confidence is low, data is missing, or a user correction conflicts with a rule?
  • How do refunds, reversals, transfers, duplicates, offline states, partial months, and shared accounts behave?

Data, Privacy, Security, and Integrations

  • What data is used, why, under what lawful/consent basis, for how long, and with which user controls?
  • Which services provide transactions, categorization, notifications, analytics, and exports, and who owns outages/reconciliation?

Accessibility and Content

  • What WCAG target and assistive-technology coverage apply?
  • How will insight language be reviewed for clarity, guilt, alarm, and financial harm?

Success and Acceptance

  • Define each metric's denominator, baseline, cohort, source, owner, target period, and guardrail.
  • Which observable success, error, permission, privacy, accessibility, and recovery cases must pass before launch?
