THE CORE IDEA
Good UX breaks complexity into simple steps. I applied the same approach to the UX process.

One monolithic prompt became a modular system of accountable parts.

AI LIMITATIONS & RESPONSIBLE USE

AI accelerates the work.
It does not upgrade weak evidence.

01
Critical decisions

Medical, legal, financial, safety, or life-changing decisions require qualified humans.

02
Private information

Credentials, personal data, medical records, and financial data stay outside casual AI workflows.

03
Unverified research

Synthetic or inferred content must never quietly become “what users said.”

02 · AI DESIGN PIPELINE

Nothing hidden.
Everything has a purpose.

Each component has a clear responsibility and shows how it contributes to the system.

01

Orchestrator

Reads state, routes the next action, and stops when human approval is required.

Coordination without losing control.
02

Phase

A bounded stage with prerequisites, ordered work, named outputs, and a review point.

Complex work becomes understandable.
03

Agent

The accountable specialist responsible for completing one phase correctly.

Clear ownership and boundaries.
04

Skill

A reusable, single-job instruction contract with inputs, outputs, and guardrails.

Repeatable quality instead of prompt luck.
05

Gate

An explicit human decision: approve, refine, or reject based on visible evidence.

Automation never silently decides readiness.
06

Artifact

A named Markdown record that preserves what was produced, known, assumed, or missing.

Every decision leaves a trace.
