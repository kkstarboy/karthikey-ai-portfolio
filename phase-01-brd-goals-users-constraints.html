6. Executive Summary

  • Three business goals are identifiable, but owners, baselines, and time horizons are incomplete.
  • The three consumer groups are provisional BRD segments; the internal product team is a supporting actor.
  • The strongest documented constraints are mobile-first usability, supportive tone, privacy explanation, security intent, performance, and categorization accuracy; most are not measurable.
  • The highest risks are incorrect categorization, privacy/trust harm, judgmental messaging, complexity, and low adoption.
  • The most promising provisional opportunities are a clear monthly summary, explainable category correction, and non-judgmental insights.
  • Overall evidence confidence: Low. The source is a single BRD with no traceable direct research, analytics dataset, stakeholder interviews, competitive evidence, or approved decision owners.

7. Completion Checklist

  • Goals, candidate metrics, groups, needs, constraints, risks, opportunities, and recommendations are captured.
  • Every material finding is labelled or described as Explicit, Inferred, Assumption, Gap, or Conflict.
  • Traceability exposes unlinked evidence, ownership, measurement, and research gaps.
  • Direct user evidence validates the segments and needs — blocked.
  • Technical/business constraints have owners and validation — blocked.
